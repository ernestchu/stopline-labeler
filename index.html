Labelling stop lines

Draw each stop line as one straight line from one end to the other, across all lanes it spans. Only the two end points count; the drawn width doesn't matter.

Phone / tabletMouse / trackpad
Draw a lineDrag with one fingerClick and drag

Select a lineTap or click it, then Delete
ZoomPinchWheel or pinch

Reset viewDouble-tapDouble-click, or 0

Next (Enter) saves the lines and jumps to the next unlabelled frame; ◀ ▶ (← →) step one frame at a time, labelled or not. No line (N) marks a frame without a stop line, Unclear (U) one you can't judge. Hard (H) flags the frame as a hard example (glare, faded paint, occlusion…) on top of its label; it doesn't move on, so flag first, then press Next, No line or Unclear. Labels stay in this browser until you press Download; send me that file.
