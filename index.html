Labelling stop lines

Draw the stop line in your own lane only, as one straight line from one lane boundary to the other. A frame has at most one stop line: drawing a new one replaces it. Only the two end points count.

Frames may arrive with a suggested label. Green: the driving model's lane boundaries. Dashed red: the line as labelled before, across all lanes. Orange: the lead car. V hides these. A frame counts once you press Next, or No line, or change it.
