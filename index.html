Reviewing windows

Each video is one window: about two seconds of driving (41 frames at 20 fps) around a frame you labelled. It loops.

The green line is your label from the labelled frame (marked in yellow at the top), carried into every other frame with the car's motion. The strip at the bottom shows every frame of the window: green a line, dark no line, grey not scored (too near the guideline's distance limits, or carried too far). The white box is the current frame.

Keep (K) the window if the green line sits on the stop line in every frame that shows one, and no frame marked "no line" shows a stop line in your lane that should count. Drop (D) it if any frame is wrong. A window is kept or dropped as a whole; nothing is edited.

After a decision the next undecided window opens. ◀ ▶ (← →) move one window at a time; ½× (S) slows the video; Space pauses. Decisions stay in this browser until you press Download; send me that file.
